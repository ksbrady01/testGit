Why we're here

“Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.”

Matthew 5:14. We want every town between the Brazos and the Hill Country to have a church that shows up: for the grieving, the new in town, the curious, and the kids who need a cheering section.

Plan a visit

Your first Sunday, start to finish.

No sign-up needed and nobody will put you on the spot. Here's what the morning looks like so nothing catches you off guard.

Get directions See service times

  • Park up front

    First-time guest spots sit by the main doors. Greeters in marigold shirts will walk you in.

  • Coffee's on us

    Fresh coffee and kolaches in the courtyard before and after every service.

  • Kids check-in

    Safe, staffed rooms for nursery through fifth grade. Check in once and get a matching pickup tag.

  • About 75 minutes

    Singing, a message, a time to respond. You'll be out in time for lunch.

  • Wear whatever

    Sunday best, jeans and boots, or gym clothes. God's looking at your heart.

  • Stay for prayer

    Our prayer team waits at the front after every service. Bring whatever you're carrying.
